I'm Coco, and everything on this site comes from me — the stays, the photographs, the opinions. This page is how I work, so you know exactly what you're reading.

## How I choose what to review

I don't review everywhere I stay, and I don't stay everywhere I'm offered. A property gets written up when it has a real sense of place and I'd send a client there. That's the bar: not the price, not the brand, but whether someone poured themselves into making it. Most properties here sit above $800 a night; a few don't, because they earned it another way.

## What a stay actually involves

I stay in the room I write about, and I tell you which one. I eat in the restaurants, I use the spa if there is one, I walk the distance to town, I check what the rate includes and what shows up on the bill. I photograph everything on film because that's how I see, and because a photograph I took is one you can trust is real. If I didn't experience something — a restaurant that was closed, a treatment I didn't book — I say so rather than describe it from the brochure.

## Firsthand versus sourced

What I saw, ate, paid and noticed is written in the first person. Facts I didn't see for myself — the architect, the opening year, room counts, a chef's background — come from the property, its press material, or an interview, and I say where. I'd rather leave a detail out than guess at it.

## Hosted stays and paid stays

Some stays I pay for. Some are hosted or discounted by the property, usually because I'm there as an advisor or photographer. Every Property piece states which. A hosted stay is reviewed exactly like a paid one: the property doesn't see the piece before it runs, doesn't approve it, and doesn't get the flaw taken out. If I couldn't write honestly, I wouldn't write it.

## The advisory practice

I also run Coco Tran Travel, where I plan trips for clients. Many of the hotels here can be booked through it, often with perks from the property's preferred-partner programmes. That relationship is stated where it's relevant. The publication and the practice are separate: you can read without ever booking, and book without ever subscribing. Nothing here is sponsored content, and a hotel can't pay to appear.

## Affiliate links

A few links earn a small commission if you book through them. It never changes what I recommend. Full details on the [Disclosures](https://cocotran.com/disclosures/) page.

## Keeping things current

Hotels change — chefs leave, pools get rebuilt, rates move. Each piece shows when I visited and when it was last updated. "Updated" means something in it actually changed; I keep the original visit date so you can judge how fresh the experience is. When I go back, the piece gets rewritten, not patched. If you know something's out of date, tell me: hello@cocotran.com.

## Photography

Every image is mine unless the caption says otherwise. Where a hotel's own photographs appear — usually in advisor roundups of places I haven't stayed — they're credited to the hotel. Alt text and captions describe what's actually in the frame. If you'd like to license a photograph, that's [Coco Tran Studio](https://cocotranstudio.com).

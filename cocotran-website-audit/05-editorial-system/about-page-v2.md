## One person, two jobs, one point of view

I'm Coco Tran. I plan trips for a living as a luxury travel advisor, and I photograph on film for a living too. This publication is where those two jobs meet: every place here has been considered the way I'd source it for a client, and seen the way I see a room through a lens. You get the practical and the beautiful in the same story, from the same person who was actually there.

## What I'm looking for

I travel for the places made with care. A boutique hotel in an odd corner of the world. A family inn passed down for generations. The genuinely luxurious property where every detail was thought about. The restaurant with the best table in town and no sign on the door. The tiny gem I stumbled on and can't stop thinking about. Expensive isn't the point; intention is. Good hospitality is supposed to make you feel something, and that's what I go looking for.

## Why you can trust what's here

- **I was there.** Every Property piece is a stay I made, in a room I name, in a month I state. Guides and routes come from trips I took or itineraries I built for clients.
- **I photograph it myself.** On film. What you see is what the place looked like the day I was in it.
- **I tell you how I paid.** Paid, hosted or discounted, it's on every review, and a hosted stay gets the same honesty as a paid one. [How we review](https://cocotran.com/how-we-review/) has the full method.

## The formats

The publication runs on a few recurring formats, and the names are part of how it works:

- **The Verdict** — a full review of a hotel, camp or lodge, room by room, from a real stay.
- **The Primer** — what to know before you book a destination: when to go, what to book first, how it works.
- **The Rolodex** — where to stay, eat and go, by neighbourhood, the way the place actually works.
- **The Route** — a day-by-day itinerary I've built and travelled.
- **The Dossier** — the practical planning file: visas, money, packing, timing.
- **The Shortlist** — quick, trusted picks on one theme.
- **Postcards and Field Notes** — written in the days after a stay, while it's still fresh.

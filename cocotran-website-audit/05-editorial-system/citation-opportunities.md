**For:** Coco Tran (cocotran.com / cocotrantravel.com) — luxury travel advisor, travel photographer, small travel publisher.
**Researched:** 24 October 2026. Every status/cost line carries a source URL and a check date. Items marked **UNVERIFIED** could not be confirmed against a primary page; treat them as leads, not facts.

## 8. What not to do

- **Paid links or "guest post" farms.** Google's link-spam policy covers buying or selling links that pass ranking credit; its site-reputation-abuse policy (enforced since May 2024, called "pay-to-play" by Google's Pandu Nayak in Nov 2025) targets third-party content placed on strong domains for ranking ([ppc.land](https://ppc.land/site-reputation-abuse/)). One manual action undoes a year of earned citations.
- **"AI-visibility" or "GEO citation" packages** that promise mentions in ChatGPT/Perplexity. No vendor controls model retrieval; the only lever is being cited on pages those systems already index (CNT, T+L, NATJA, Wikidata, real press).
- **Pay-to-appear podcasts and pay-to-feature "Top 50 advisors" lists.** They read as advertising to editors and to models.
- **Full cross-posting** to Substack/LinkedIn articles without canonical control; it can outrank the original.
- **Mass HARO/SOS replies** off-topic; SOS removes spammers, and reporters remember names.
- **Fake expertise.** Only answer queries Coco has booked, shot or stayed.

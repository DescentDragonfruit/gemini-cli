# Marketing playbook — how every piece and post earns its keep

The desk acts as Coco's marketing director. Every article and every post carries a marketing read: who it is for, the decision it settles, the one call to action that fits, where it travels, and the number that tells us it worked. This is the standard the reads are written against.

## The business, in one line
Readers arrive with a decision (where to stay, which of two, is it worth it). The magazine settles it with first-hand intel. The money is made when that reader books through Coco Tran Travel (same rate, her perks) or joins the Planning Room. Everything else (saves, follows, citations) is a step toward one of those two.

## The funnel, and which pieces live where
| Stage | What the reader wants | Pieces | The ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | an answer, fast | Answer, Primer, pins, reels | save / follow / read the piece |
| Decide | the comparison, the shortlist | Comparison, Shortlist, Rolodex, Roundup | the Planning Room (map, sheet, trip file) |
| Book | the right room, the perks | Verdict, Trade-Off | book it through me |
| Trust | the method, the person | Field Notes, Postcard, How we review, About | subscribe to the Insider |

Rule: one primary ask per piece, matched to the stage. A second ask may appear once, at the end, never mid-text.

## The five moves that move the needle
1. **Own the decision query.** Titles in the lock pattern, the short answer first, the glance rows. A piece that answers "X vs Y" or "is X worth it" earns the AI citation and the click.
2. **Put the booking box where the decision lands.** On a Verdict: after the room section, with the room to ask for. On a Comparison: after "Who books which". Never in the first screen.
3. **Give the Planning Room something real.** The trip file, the saved map, the booking-order sheet. The sign-up line names the exact file.
4. **Distribute every piece five ways within 14 days.** Carousel (saves), reel or Edits video (reach), pin (search, lasts a year), a story set with a question (replies), an email to the list (bookings). The desk's Social studio builds all five from one piece.
5. **Close the loop.** Reader questions become Answer pieces. Inquiries carry their source. The weekly rankings run says which pieces produce bookings, and those get the next update.

## Calls to action, the exact lines
- Book: "Book it through me. Same rate as the hotel, plus [perk, perk], and I'll ask for [the room]." → cocotrantravel.com/contact?hotel=&source=&src=booking-box
- Planning Room: "The full trip file (day by day, booking order, the map) is in the Planning Room." → one line, once.
- Insider: "I write the Insider when something changes: a rate, a chef, a room worth moving to." → trust pieces only.
- Never: "click here", "don't miss", "limited time", discount language, or two asks in one screen.

## Hooks that work for this audience (luxury planners, 35–60, booking 3–9 months out)
- A number with a decision in it: "Two lagoons, one honeymoon. Here is how I chose."
- The room: "Ask for suite 4. Here is why."
- The honest trade: "The Maldives wins on choice. Bora Bora wins on the view."
- The insider price frame: "$3,500 a person, five nights, March. Worth it, and here is the math."
- Avoid: generic awe, "hidden gem", listicles without a verdict, anything that could be any blog.

## Distribution checklist per piece (the desk prints this on every piece)
- [ ] Carousel built to be saved (Tue/Thu 7–9am PT)
- [ ] Reel or Edits video (Sat 10am PT) · YouTube cut when the piece is a Route or Dossier
- [ ] Pin with the search phrase as the title
- [ ] Story set ending on a question sticker
- [ ] Insider email (Verdicts and Comparisons only) with the booking line
- [ ] Pitched to one outlet or newsletter if the piece carries a first (new opening, a reopening, a rate change)
- [ ] Internal links from the three best siblings (autolink does the first mention; the worker adds the rest)
- [ ] Hub page updated

## Social cadence that compounds
- One post a day at most. Carousel → reel → pin → story, in that order, over seven days from the same piece.
- Captions: first line stands alone, one real question at the end. Hashtags in the first comment, five, specific.
- DM keyword only with a real deliverable behind it. The DM reply links the piece and offers the call.
- Boosting: only a carousel that already earned saves above 3% in 48 hours, to lookalikes of site visitors, never cold.

## Email
- Welcome sequence (built): who Coco is, how she reviews, one Verdict, one Comparison, the booking line.
- Insider: when something changes, not on a schedule. Subject lines name the hotel.
- Every email carries one link to a piece and one line to book through her.

## Measurement, the only numbers that matter
| Piece type | Watch | Good |
|---|---|---|
| Verdict | booking-box clicks → inquiries with that source | 1 inquiry per 150 clicks |
| Comparison / Shortlist | Planning Room sign-ups from the piece | 1 per 100 readers |
| Answer / Primer | AI citations, position in Search Console | cited within 60 days |
| Social | saves per reach, profile taps → link clicks | saves ≥ 3% of reach |
| Email | replies and booking-line clicks | 2% click to the booking line |

## The weekly marketing move
Each Monday the desk names one move for the week from the data: the piece closest to page one that needs a title, the Verdict with clicks and no inquiries that needs its booking box moved, the season that starts next month, the trip to pre-announce. One move, done well, beats five started.

# 90-day plan and what to measure

Supersedes nothing in `02-action-plan.md` — it sequences it. Fix the current experience first; publish into a sound structure second; grow third.

## Days 1–14 · Clean up and baseline

- [ ] Phase 1 of `02-action-plan.md` in full: subscribe-page errors, placeholders, Kimpton intro, sitemap, noindex, feed, 404, schema per type, site identity.
- [ ] Publish `/how-we-review/` and the About v2.
- [ ] Titles/intros on the 10 most important pieces (6 Properties + Japan, Kyoto, Tokyo, Santorini Primers): answerable-opening block + title rewrites.
- [ ] Search Console + GA4 confirmed; GA4 custom channel group "AI referrals" (regex on referrer: `chatgpt\.com|openai\.com|perplexity\.ai|claude\.ai|copilot\.microsoft\.com|gemini\.google\.com|you\.com`).
- [ ] **Record the baseline**: organic clicks/impressions (16 months), pages indexed, newsletter subscribers, paid members by tier, advisory inquiries/month, the AI benchmark (`07-ai-benchmark/2026-10-24-baseline.md`).

## Days 15–45 · Destination authority

- [ ] Paywall decision made and implemented (Primers free; markup everywhere).
- [ ] Four hubs built: Japan (+Tokyo, Kyoto, Kanazawa, Takayama), Mallorca, Mexico City, Vietnam/Hanoi — per `03-fixes/destination-hub-spec.md`.
- [ ] Answerable-opening block live in the templates; backfill done for Properties, Primers, Rolodexes.
- [ ] Coco Photo CMS plugin installed; bulk WebP regenerate; alt-text sweep.
- [ ] Competing pages resolved (Mexico City hotels, Lake Como ×3, Hanoi ×5, Fuji ×2).
- [ ] Free-to-paid transition redesigned: TOC visible above the gate, sample pieces on `/subscribe/`.

## Days 46–90 · Visibility and conversion

- [ ] 8–12 "The Answer" pieces from the question bank (2/week), starting with the questions where a free piece doesn't exist (#1, 3, 9, 13, 14, 16, 19).
- [ ] Two new comparisons (Takayama vs Kanazawa; two Mallorca properties) — the format AI engines quote most.
- [ ] Benchmark run #2 (day ~60) and #3 (day ~90); compare to baseline.
- [ ] Citation routine running weekly (`citation-opportunities.md`); 2 pitches/week; log in the Editorial Desk tracker.
- [ ] Review which pieces drove members and inquiries; double down on that cluster.
- [ ] Old-post programme: ~15 respun/merged/retired.

No traffic target is set until the day-14 baseline exists. Set the day-90 targets then, against the baseline, per outcome below.

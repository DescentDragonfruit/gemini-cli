# Social reuse playbook — one piece, one spine, every channel

Builds on the existing **Content Spinner Skill** (Notion, March 2026): the insider detail is the spine and never changes; format, length and opening angle change. The Coco Edit (80–95 words, Hook → Reveal → Insider intel → Takeaway → "Follow for more intel on luxury travel."), the carousel (5–7 slides, caption 3–5 sentences, ≤5 specific hashtags), the YouTube Short (~120 words) and the banned-words list all carry over. What's added here: the saves-and-engagement rules, the comment/DM mechanics, the on-brand hashtag sets, and the hand-off from a published piece to a kit — which the Studio Desk's **Social** tab generates and stores.

## What every kit contains (from one article)

| Output | Spec | Optimised for |
|---|---|---|
| **Carousel** (Instagram, 8–10 slides) | Slide 1 hook (≤12 words, a claim or a number, no hotel name); slides 2–8 one fact or one decision each (≤25 words), in the order a planner needs them; slide 9 "the one most people miss"; slide 10 takeaway + save prompt + where the full piece lives. Photo note per slide. | **Saves** — carousels that are a usable checklist get saved; "pretty" ones get liked |
| **Caption** | 3–5 sentences in Coco's voice; first line stands alone (it's what shows); the "why" in sentence 2–3; a question or a DM prompt last. No hashtags in the caption. | Comments |
| **First comment** | The hashtags (5, specific) + the DM keyword line if used | Reach without cluttering the caption |
| **DM keyword** (when it earns it) | "Comment **ROOM** and I'll send you the room to ask for" — only when the piece has a concrete deliverable to send (room number, the trip file, the shortlist PDF, a booking link with perks). Never for a plain article link. | Comments + DMs → leads |
| **Coco Edit script** (Reel / Short) | 80–95 words, existing formula; on-screen text for the hook; b-roll list from the piece's photos | Reach |
| **Story set** (3–5 frames) | Hook frame → 2–3 fact frames → poll or question sticker → link sticker to the piece | Engagement, link clicks |
| **Pinterest** | Title ≤100 chars (the search phrase), description 2 sentences with the keyword, board | Evergreen search traffic |
| **Alt text** per image | What's actually in the frame + place; no keyword lists | Accessibility, image search |
| **Tags** | The property's account, the destination tourism board, the photographer credit (herself), a partner if hosted | Reach via the tagged accounts |

## The rules that make it get saved and shared

1. **Lead with the decision, not the place.** "Oia or Imerovigli: the honest answer" beats "Santorini ✨". The hook promises something the viewer will want to come back to.
2. **One idea per slide; one slide per decision.** If a reader could screenshot slide 4 and plan from it, it's right.
3. **Numbers and names.** Room 312. €1,400 in June. 6:30 at Fushimi Inari. Specifics are what people save.
4. **The "most people miss" slide** is the share trigger — the thing they'll send to the friend they're travelling with.
5. **Say "save this for [trip]"** on the last slide, plainly. It works, and it's not off-brand when the content earned it.
6. **Ask one question in the caption** that a real traveller would answer ("Which would you pick — the pool or the beach?"). Don't ask "thoughts?".
7. **Reply to every comment in the first hour** in her voice; the first-hour replies are what the algorithm reads.
8. **DM keyword only with a real deliverable** (above). Over-use kills it.
9. **Hosted stays say so** in the caption ("hosted by the property; opinions mine") — same honesty as the site.
10. **Never the banned words**; never "link in bio" as the whole CTA (say what the link gives).

## On-brand hashtag sets (pick one set, ≤5, specific)

- **Hotel/Verdict:** `#[hotelname]` `#[hotelbrand]` (e.g. `#belmond`) `#[place]hotels` `#luxuryhotelreview` `#travelwithanadvisor`
- **Destination/Rolodex/Primer:** `#[place]travel` `#[place]guide` `#[country]luxurytravel` `#firsttimein[place]` `#traveladvisor`
- **Route/itinerary:** `#[place]itinerary` `#[N]daysin[place]` `#luxuryitinerary` `#[country]travel` `#travelplanning`
- **Food/Shortlist:** `#[place]restaurants` `#[place]food` `#wheretoeat[place]` `#[dish]` `#traveladvisor`
- **Photography/Postcard:** `#filmphotography` `#[place]onfilm` `#travelphotographer` `#[place]` `#cocotranstudio`
- Never: `#wanderlust` `#travelgram` `#instatravel` `#luxurylifestyle` `#bucketlist` or any tag with 50M+ posts.

## Comment and DM behaviour

- Pin a comment that adds one more fact (not "link in bio").
- When someone asks "which room / which hotel / when", answer in the comment **and** offer the DM: "Sent you the details — and the perks if you book it through me."
- Every DM that asks about a booking gets the booking-box link (same rate + perks), not the membership page.

## Cadence (from the Spinner, kept)

Day 1 Reel · Day 3 Carousel · Day 7 Short · Day 14 the piece goes in the newsletter · ongoing Pinterest. Stories on the day of each.

## Hand-off

Studio Desk → Library → open the piece → **Make social**. The kit is generated from the piece's own text and photos list, saved under `social/<id>`, and marked on the library record. Nothing is posted automatically.

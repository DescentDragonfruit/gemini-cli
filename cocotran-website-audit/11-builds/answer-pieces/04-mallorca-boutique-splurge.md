# Which boutique hotels in Mallorca are worth the splurge?

## Short answer

Belmond La Residencia in Deià is the one: two sixteenth- and seventeenth-century manor houses in an olive grove above the village, donkeys on the terraces, and the hotel I stayed a week at and still think about. Son Bunyola, Richard Branson's 1,300-acre estate down the coast between Banyalbufar and Deià, is the splurge for a group who want the villas and nobody else around, and Sa Pedrissa, a six-room finca with a pool on the cliff edge a few minutes along from Deià, is my choice for a couple who want no one around at all. Base in the mountains and come down to the sea; that is the whole strategy.

## Why

I came up the Tramuntana road from Palma with the sea appearing and disappearing on my left, and La Residencia does not show itself until the last bend. You are above the village rather than in it, which gives you the view back at Deià and a five-minute walk down whenever you want it. I had a Deluxe Junior Suite for a week in July [VERIFY: year], and the detail I remember most is the terracotta floors staying cool all day, which in a Mallorcan July is the detail that matters most. El Olivo, in the five-hundred-year-old olive press, was the best dinner of the week.

So much is included once you are there: the complimentary boat trip, the donkey walk through the olive terraces with a picnic at the end, the gardens, the kids' club. From around five hundred euros in the quiet months it is one of the better values in the Belmond portfolio [VERIFY: `rate_from` field reads $550; reconcile currency]. In August the maths is harder, and the answer depends on whether a week of doing very little in an olive grove is your idea of a holiday. Mine, it turns out, is.

Son Bunyola opened in 2023 on a working finca with the sea a long way below, and the three villas on the grounds are the version to take if there are enough of you [VERIFY: stayed, or advisor-sourced; say so on the page]. Sa Pedrissa is the opposite scale, six rooms and a cliff pool, for people who want the valley to themselves [VERIFY: stayed, or advisor-sourced].

Two things are worth knowing before you book La Residencia. The estate is steep, so flag any mobility issue and ask for the main building. And the nearest swim is a forty-minute walk down to Cala Deià and a longer one back up, which makes for a lovely morning rather than a beach holiday.

## If you're choosing

- **La Residencia** if you want a place rather than a resort: honeymooners, families, anyone who photographs anything.
- **Son Bunyola** if there are enough of you for one of the three villas and the point is the estate around it.
- **Sa Pedrissa** if you are two and want silence, a cliff pool and dinner in the village.
- **Cap Rocat**, the fortress cut into the cliff across the bay from Palma, if you want the extraordinary and a city within reach [VERIFY: stayed, or advisor-sourced].
- **Book the hire car before the hotel** for August; it genuinely sells out first.

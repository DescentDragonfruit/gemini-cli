Hi [first name],

Thank you for signing up. Before anything else, I want you to know how this place works, because it's the whole reason it exists.

Every hotel review here comes from a stay I made. I tell you the room I slept in, the month I was there, and whether I paid, was hosted, or got a discount. A hosted stay gets the same honesty as a paid one; the property doesn't see the review first, and the flaw doesn't come out.

I'm also a travel advisor and a film photographer, which means every place gets looked at twice: the way I'd source it for a client, and the way I see a room through a lens. The photographs are mine, taken on film, on the day.

If you want the full method, it's one page: [How we review](https://cocotran.com/how-we-review/).

Next time I'll send you the piece that shows what that looks like in practice: a room, a pool, and the thing that consistently missed.

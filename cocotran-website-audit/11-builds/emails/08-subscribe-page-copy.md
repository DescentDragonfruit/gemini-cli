## Three ways to use this

**The Reader — free**
Every review, guide, shortlist, postcard and answer, in full. The newsletter. No meter, no wall on the reading.

**The Planning Room — $100 / year or $12 / month**
Everything free, plus the planning layer: each route and dossier as a trip file (day-by-day, booking-order sheet with lead times, a map of every place named); Private Intel three times a year; a members' question answered each month; early access to new reviews. Cancel any time; access to the end of your term.

**The Insider — $200 / year**
Everything in The Planning Room, plus the bridge to the advisory practice: priority access when you're ready to plan, a 20-minute trip call, and a $200 credit toward your Coco Tran Travel planning fee, which makes The Insider net-free for anyone who books a trip. Complimentary for active Coco Tran Travel clients.

## Book through me, whatever you choose

Any high-end hotel on this site can be booked through Coco Tran Travel at the hotel's own rate. Through the preferred-partner programmes my clients typically enjoy a room upgrade when available, daily breakfast, a hotel credit and flexible check-in, wherever the programme applies. No membership needed. [Request a booking →](https://cocotrantravel.com/contact)

Hi [first name],

A lot of people who read my reviews plan their own trips, and I love that. So here's the one thing I'd want a friend to know.

When you book a high-end hotel through me, you pay the hotel's own rate, the same one you'd see on its website. What changes is what comes with it: through the preferred-partner programmes (Four Seasons Preferred Partner, Belmond Bellini Club, Virtuoso and the others), my clients typically enjoy a room upgrade when one's available, daily breakfast, a hotel credit, and early check-in or late check-out, wherever the programme applies. The hotel pays me, not you.

That's it. No membership, no planning fee for a single hotel. You send me the hotel and the dates; I confirm the booking and the perks in writing.

**How we review.**
- Every Verdict comes from a real stay — never a site inspection alone, never a press release.
- We state the room we stayed in, the month, and whether the stay was paid, discounted or hosted. Hosted stays are reviewed exactly as paid ones; the property does not see the review before publication.
- Guides and routes are built from trips we took or itineraries we planned for clients, and are updated when we return.
- Where a hotel can be booked through Coco Tran Travel, we say so. Membership and advisory are separate; you can read without booking, and book without subscribing.

## FAQ

**What's included in The Property?**
Full hotel, camp and lodge reviews — real stays, photographed and assessed room by room. Full-length reviews, quick takes on new openings, and side-by-side comparisons when we're weighing two properties against each other.

**What's included in The Guide?**
Complete destination intel — the one document you'd want before planning a trip somewhere new. The Primer (what to know before you book), The Rolodex (where to stay, eat and go, by neighbourhood) and focused city guides for the places we know especially well.

**What's included in The Brief?**
Real trip stories, start to finish — how an itinerary came together, what changed along the way, and how it played out. The Route (day-by-day) and The Dossier (the planning file for a destination).

**What's included in The Shortlist?**
Destination picks and quick guides — for when you want a fast, trusted answer instead of a full breakdown. Black books, best-of lists, and quick picks by category.

**What's included in The Dispatch?**
The reporting written closest to the ground: postcards from a recent stay, written in the days after checking out; field notes; and longer essays on where travel is headed.

**What's included in The Coco Edit?**
Short-form video — destination reels, hotel walkthroughs, and behind-the-scenes clips from the road.

**What's the difference between The Reader, The Archive and The Insider?**
The Reader is free, always, and covers The Dispatch, The Shortlist, The Coco Edit and the newsletter. The Archive ($100/yr or $12/mo) unlocks the full library — every property review, every guide, every route. The Insider ($200/yr) includes everything in The Archive plus direct advisory access and the perks that come with it.

**What exactly do I get with The Archive?**
Full access to The Property, The Guide, The Brief, The Dispatch, The Shortlist and The Coco Edit, plus the newsletter. Nothing metered, nothing paywalled once you're in.

**What does The Insider add that The Archive doesn't?**
Priority advisory access when you're ready to plan a trip; VIP amenities on high-end hotels booked through Coco Tran Travel; a $200 credit toward your Coco Tran Travel planning fee; and Private Intel — reporting reserved for Insiders, three times a year.

**I'm already a Coco Tran Travel client — do I get Insider access automatically?**
⚠ *Confirm the mechanism.* Yes — The Insider is complimentary for active travel clients. You'll receive a client code for one year of access.

**How does the VIP hotel amenity perk work?**
When you book a high-end hotel through Coco Tran Travel as an Insider, we add amenities where the property allows: a room upgrade, a $100 hotel credit, early check-in, late check-out, and a welcome amenity. Availability varies by property.

**Do the Insider hotel amenities apply if I book independently?**
No. The amenities come through Coco Tran Travel's relationships with each property, so they apply only to stays booked through Coco Tran Travel. A hotel booked on your own won't carry them.

**What is "priority advisory access"?**
As an Insider you go to the front of the line when you're ready to plan — direct time with the advisory team, ahead of general inquiries.

**Can I pay monthly instead of annually?**
The Archive is $12/month or $100/year. The Insider is annual only, at $200/year.

**Can I switch The Archive from monthly to annual billing?**
⚠ *Confirm the policy.* Yes. From your account page, change your plan to annual; the switch takes effect at your next renewal and you'll be billed $100 for the year from that date. *(If the membership plugin doesn't support plan switching, use instead: "Not yet from the account page — email hello@cocotran.com and we'll move you across at your next renewal.")*

**Can I cancel anytime, and is there a refund?**
You can cancel anytime. We don't offer refunds or proration, and you keep full access through the end of your current billing term — no early cutoff.

**If I cancel, when do I lose access?**
At the end of your current billing period. Nothing is cut off early.

**How often is new content added?**
The Property, The Guide, The Brief, The Dispatch, The Shortlist and The Coco Edit publish on an ongoing basis. Private Intel arrives three times a year for Insiders.

**If I cancel my Insider membership, do I lose the $200 planning credit?**
No. The credit toward a Coco Tran Travel planning fee stays available to use after your membership ends.

**How is a membership different from booking a trip with Coco Tran Travel?**
Membership is the reporting and content — this publication. Coco Tran Travel is the trip-planning service. The Insider tier is the bridge between the two (advisory access, the credit), but they're separate: you can read without booking, and book without subscribing.

**Is this a gift?**
Memberships can be bought for yourself or as a gift. Choose "Gift" at checkout and we'll send the recipient their access.

## The rule for every old intro

1. **Sentence 1:** what it is + where (one keyword mention, naturally).
2. **Sentence 2:** when you went, how (room/route/season), what the piece covers.
3. **Short-version box:** the answer, in 2–3 sentences — book it if / skip it if.
4. **Dates + disclosure line.**
5. No "(2026)", no "ultimate", no "lol", no "look no further", no "#1 TOP".
